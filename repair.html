    Repairs

    Get the newest version
    Throws away the copy kept on this phone and downloads the app again from the website. Your work is not touched.
    Reload data from online
    Forgets what this phone thinks it has downloaded, so the next open loads everything fresh from the online database. Changes not yet sent are kept.
    Sign out on this phone
    Clears the sign-in only. Work stays on the phone and is sent after you sign in again.
    Download a backup
    Everything kept on this phone — settings, orders, flats, accounts — as one file. Keep it on your computer or Google Drive.
    Problem report
    The results above plus the phone details, to send to the admin or to Claude.
    Full reset
    Removes everything the app kept on this phone and starts it fresh, like a new phone. A backup file is downloaded first. Use only when nothing else helps and your work is already online.
    Full reset — are you sure?
    Type RESET to confirm. A backup is downloaded first. Anything not yet sent online will be lost.

    When something goes wrong

    The app opens blank or says "could not start"
    1. Open the link in Chrome (on iPhone: Safari) — not inside WhatsApp or Facebook. In WhatsApp tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome.
    2. Update Chrome from the Play Store.
    3. Press Update above (newest version).
    4. Still blank: Make report and send it to the admin.
    "New version" never shows / old screens / a button is missing
    1. Press Update above. The check "Website version" shows whether this phone is behind.
    2. If the website itself is behind, the admin uploads the new index.html and sw.js to GitHub (both files).
    "Please sign in again" / "Session expired"
    1. Normal after a long time away or a password change. Open the app and sign in — your work waits on the phone and is sent after sign-in.
    2. If it keeps coming back every few minutes: check the Clock row above — a wrong phone time breaks sign-ins. Phone Settings → Date & time → Automatic.
    3. Not on the allowed list? The admin adds your email: Settings → Users.
    "Can't reach the online database"
    1. Check the internet — open any website. Mobile data with a weak signal often fails while Wi-Fi works.
    2. Keep working: everything is saved on the phone and sent when the database is back.
    3. Over an hour: the admin opens supabase.com → the project. If it says Paused, press Restore project and wait a few minutes.
    Changes "not sent online yet" for a long time
    1. Check the row Changes waiting above and the internet.
    2. Open the app and tap Send now on the yellow bar, or wait — it retries by itself.
    3. If a sign-in problem is shown, sign in again first.
    4. Never do a full reset while changes are waiting — they would be lost. Download a backup first.
    Another phone does not see my order / flat
    1. On your phone: the change must be sent (no yellow bar). On the other phone: pull down or reopen the app — it refreshes every 45 seconds.
    2. Both phones must be signed in to the same online database (check the row Online database).
    3. Still different: Reload data from online on the phone that is behind.
    🎤 Voice typing or 👥 contacts does not work
    1. Voice typing needs Google Chrome on Android (the Google app does the listening) or Safari on iPhone. Inside other apps it is hidden.
    2. Allow the microphone: phone Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Microphone.
    3. Contacts picking works only in Chrome on Android. Otherwise type the name and number.
    4. The keyboard's own 🎤 key always works as a fallback.
    📷 Photos do not upload / "waiting to upload"
    1. Photos upload in the background when there is internet; Wi-Fi is faster.
    2. Check the row Phone storage: a full phone cannot keep photos. Free space, then reopen the app.
    3. Allow Camera and Photos for Chrome if taking a photo fails.
    "Phone storage is full"
    1. Free space on the phone (old videos, WhatsApp media). The app needs only a little, but Chrome refuses to save when the phone is nearly full.
    2. Your work is still saved online while signed in.
    Morning reminders do not arrive
    1. Allow Notifications for Chrome and for the installed app; turn off battery saving for Chrome.
    2. In the app: Settings → Phone → Morning reminders → turn on for this phone.
    3. On iPhone the app must be installed on the Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and iOS 16.4 or newer.
    I forgot my password
    1. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? Email me a reset link.
    2. Or the admin sets a new one: Settings → Users → 🔑 next to your email.
    Something was deleted or wrong data appeared
    1. Only an admin can delete. The admin can bring a deleted order back: Settings → Backup → Online copies → Restore deleted.
    2. A whole day's copy can be restored from Online copies (kept 21 days).
    3. A backup file from Download a backup can be restored in the app: Settings → Backup → Restore backup.

    For the admin: Supabase project health, logs and the daily check are at supabase.com → the project; the daily check also runs from GitHub → Actions → "Daily database check".
